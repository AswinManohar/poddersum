version: verdict-v1

Task: help a listener decide whether this episode is worth their time.

Write claims in three sections, each claim marked with its "section":
- "who_its_for": 1 to 2 claims on who will get the most out of this episode and why.
- "whats_new": 1 to 3 claims on what is new, surprising or uncommon in it, compared with
  what most people already know about the topic.
- "best_10_minutes": exactly 1 claim naming the best stretch of about 10 minutes to
  listen to if they only have 10 minutes, and what they will get from it. Cite exactly
  two segments: the one where the stretch starts and the one where it ends.

Every claim cites the segments that support it.

Output format: a JSON object with a "claims" list. Each claim has "section", "text" and
"segment_ids".
